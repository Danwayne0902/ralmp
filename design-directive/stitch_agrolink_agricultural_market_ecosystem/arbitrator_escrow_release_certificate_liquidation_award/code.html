fact_check 2. Forensic Findings & Legal Grounds

WHEREAS, Complainant lodged an escrow freeze request on 21 October 2025 regarding Soya Beans Lot #792 (30 Metric Tons), claiming delivery moisture content of 14.2% exceeding the maximum contract threshold of 10.5%.

FORENSIC EXAMINATION: Telematics from AgroLink Weighbridge and Quality Lab Sensor Pack #QL-881 verified that goods departed Gboko certified at 9.8% moisture. An independent survey verified heavy torrential thunderstorm ingress along the Ore–Sagamu transit corridor resulting from tarpaulin failure on carrier vehicle Tarkaa DKA-481-XA.

RULING: Pursuant to RAMLP Operational Guideline Art. 14(3) and the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, Seller completed good faith dispatch. The loss is determined to be a transit casualty covered under Sovereign Trust Marine Transit Cargo Policy #STP-CARGO-771. Complainant is awarded remediation compensation from the transit insurance pool, whilst Seller’s Milestone 2 funds are exonerated and declared ripe for immediate 100% liquidation.
